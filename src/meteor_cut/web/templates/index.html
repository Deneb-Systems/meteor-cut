Meteor Cut

Upload a night-sky video from a camera on a tripod. Meteor Cut finds every meteor with Sky Tracker, makes a highlight video with only them, sorted by speed, and gives you pictures and tables. Everything runs on this computer.

0 level, 90 straight up. Needed for km/s.
In the video:

Without a lens, speeds are in pixels per second. With the lens, in degrees per second; with the tilt too, an estimate in km/s (the part of the speed across your view, so the real speed is at least that).
